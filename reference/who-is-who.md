# Who is who

One job: who works at Steady Dose, what each person owns, and who to ask about what.

Three people work at Steady Dose, plus you. You do day one with the onboarder; the three of them are who you ask when it can't answer, or when something is theirs to decide. Everyone is reached on Google Chat in your Steady Dose Google account. There is no phone list; if you need a number, ask Marcus.

## The people

**Braden Crocco, PharmD: owner and lead tutor.** He started Steady Dose, and you report to him. He works full time as a hospital pharmacist, so he's on Chat in the evenings and on weekends, and a message during the day gets an answer that night.
He owns: how we teach and the methods; the library of sheets and keys; anything that goes to a student (he approves every send); the policies and the workspace itself; new students, the free consult and marketing.

**Marcus Lee: operations and scheduling.** He handles the business side day to day.
He owns: your pay, hours, contract and tax forms; logins and accounts; the calendar and booking links; a student who wants to reschedule; payments and refunds (he processes them); the shared inbox.

**Priya Raman, PharmD: senior tutor.** She's been tutoring here the longest after Braden, and she's who you work with day to day in your first weeks.
She owns: your shadowing from day two, and checking your gradings of real homework; how to teach a topic that isn't landing; grading questions; a student who is stuck, upset or falling behind.

## Who to ask

| If it's about... | Ask |
|---|---|
| your pay, pay dates, hours, your contract, a tax form | Marcus |
| a login or your booking page that doesn't work, or a login you don't have | Marcus |
| a student who wants to move or cancel a session, turns up late, or doesn't show | Marcus |
| homework a student says they sent that isn't in their folder | Marcus |
| you're sick, or running late for your own day | Marcus, and tell Priya if you were shadowing her |
| Marcus isn't answering and it can't wait | Braden |
| a payment, a refund, a charge a student asks about | Marcus (and he checks with Braden) |
| the rebate, or the exit call after a student's exam | Marcus |
| how to explain a topic, or how a method works | Priya first, then Braden |
| a fact about the exam itself, such as the passing score or the format | Priya |
| a grading question, or whether an answer counts | Priya |
| the session you were meant to shadow got cancelled | Priya; she'll use a past session's run sheet and record instead |
| a key that looks wrong | Braden, and tell Priya too |
| a student who is stuck, upset or far behind | Priya, and she brings in Braden if needed |
| anything going to a student: an email, homework, a recap | Braden (you write a draft; he approves and sends it) |
| a new worksheet or method sheet, or a change to one | Braden |
| a policy, or a page in the workspace, that seems wrong or missing | Braden |
| a student who asks about their own health or medicines | don't answer it; tell them to ask their own pharmacist or doctor, then tell Braden |
| anything else, or you're not sure | Braden |

If the person you need is out, ask whoever is next on that row. If nobody can answer, it waits; don't guess.
