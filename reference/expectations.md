# What we expect, and when

One job: what Steady Dose expects of a new tutor and when, from day one to three months, and what nobody expects yet.

Nobody scores you. We work with a new tutor the way we work with a student (`how-we-teach.md`): Priya puts what you did next to what was asked, and starts with what you got right.

The job is part time: about 15 to 20 hours a week. Your own schedule is Marcus's (`who-is-who.md`).

## Day one and week one

| When | Expected | Not expected yet |
|---|---|---|
| Day one | You start the six parts with the onboarder, at your own pace. What you don't reach is still there afterwards (`first-day.md`) | Finishing in one sitting, remembering the methods, or handing anything in |
| Week one | You shadow two or three of Priya's sessions, and you grade real homework with Priya checking | Running a session, or drafting anything for a student |

## The steps to your own students

There are four. The first two start together on day two. Braden decides when you move on to step 3 and to step 4, and this is what he looks for.

1. **Shadow two or three of Priya's sessions.**
2. **Grade real homework, with Priya checking.** Before step 3, your gradings agree with hers on the answer and on what happened. A different calc-CARE step from hers is fine if you can say how it catches the miss (`practice-homework.md`).
3. **Run a session with Priya on the call.** Before step 4, you did the six things from "What to look for" in that session (`sample-session.md`). It doesn't have to run to the minute.
4. **Run your first session on your own.** That's your first student, usually around week three.

## Your first three months

- A short check-in with Braden at one month, and another at three months.
- By three months, you write your own run sheets. Until then, Priya writes the run sheet for each session you run.
- By three months, you also draft recap and homework emails for Braden to approve and send.
- Not expected, then or later: writing or changing a worksheet, a method sheet or a key, or deciding anything about money. The library is Braden's, and money goes to Marcus (`who-is-who.md`).

## Always, from day two

- Homework is graded before its session.
- Nothing goes to a student without Braden. He approves and sends every email, homework and recap.
- When you run a session, you write its record the same day.
- When you're not sure, you ask. Nobody expects you to guess.
