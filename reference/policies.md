# Policies

One job: the business rules a new tutor needs to know, in short. When a student asks about money or terms, you don't decide anything; you know the answer or you send them to the right person.

## What we sell

| Offer | Sessions | Price | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free consult | 1 × 15 min | free | every new student starts here; Braden runs it |
| Math Package | 3 × 60 min | $289 | the NAPLEX math: calculations, biostatistics, PK, diabetes math |
| Full NAPLEX | 5 × 60 min | $749 | the whole exam, math and clinical, with a study plan |
| Add-on session | 1 × 60 min | $95 | only for students who have bought a package |
| Add-on four-pack | 4 × 60 min | $360 | the same, four sessions |

Every session is 60 minutes, one-on-one, on Google Meet. Questions about buying go to Braden; questions about a charge go to Marcus.

## The package window

A student uses all their sessions **before they sit the exam, or within 12 weeks of enrolling, whichever comes first.** Add-on sessions run on the window of the package they follow. When the last session is held or the window closes, the package is done.

## Homework

Every session has homework. It's due **12 hours before the session starts**, so the tutor has time to grade it and write the run sheet.

## The money-back guarantee

We never promise a pass or a score. But if a student does all three of these and honestly doesn't feel more prepared, they get a full refund:

1. Comes to their sessions.
2. Sends their homework before each session.
3. Finishes inside the package window.

If a student asks for a refund, don't promise or refuse anything. Tell Marcus; he and Braden decide.

## The rebate (later, not day one)

After their exam, a student can earn back $50 (Math Package) or $100 (Full NAPLEX) by booking a short exit call and filling in two short forms within 72 hours. Marcus handles it. If a student asks, say it's real and that Marcus will walk them through it.

## Session notes and recording

Before the first session, every student signs a consent form, kept in their `paperwork/` folder. Written notes from each session's audio are a condition of tutoring. Video recording is optional; the student chooses on the form. **Before you turn on video recording, check their form.** If you can't find it, don't record, and tell Marcus.

## What we never say

These apply to everything: a session, an email, a chat message.

- We never promise a pass, a score or a result, in any words. "More prepared" is as far as we go.
- We teach; we don't practice pharmacy. No advice about a real patient, or about the student's own health or medicines.
- We're not connected to the exam board, any board of pharmacy, or any school, and we never suggest we are.
- In anything public (a post, a website, a review reply), we don't name another prep company. In a private session or email, a student can name what they use; you can talk about it.
- Students' details stay private. Never share one student's work, score or name with another.

## Not written down here

There's no written policy in this folder on late arrivals, no-shows or how much notice a reschedule needs. If a student asks, tell them Marcus handles scheduling and you'll pass it on.
