# Your first day

One job: what happens on a new tutor's first day, in order, and what waits until later.

Day one is practice and watching. You won't touch money, send anything to a student, or work with a real student on your own. Nothing you do today can go wrong in a way that matters.

Steady Dose has no office. Day one is on Google Meet, like everything else here. This page gives the order of the day, not clock times: Braden tells you the start time and the times for each part, breaks included. If you don't have them, ask Marcus.

## The day

| When | What | With |
|---|---|---|
| First hour | Your logins, the workspace included (see `tools-and-logins.md`), and a few questions about what you already know: the NAPLEX, tutoring, folders and AI | Marcus |
| Morning | How we teach: the why, the methods, how the sheets build, how a session runs (`how-we-teach.md`) | Braden |
| Late morning | The workspace: what each folder name means, the folders you'll touch, the `awaiting-…` rule (`the-workspace.md`) | Braden |
| Midday | Shadow one of Priya's sessions, live if one is booked, or from a past session's run sheet and record | Priya |
| Afternoon | Grade one practice homework by hand against its key. `the-workspace.md` says where the homework, the key and your grading go | Priya shows you what a finished grading looks like, then checks yours |
| End of day | Anything you want to ask again, then what's next for you tomorrow | the onboarder for your questions, Braden for tomorrow |

## Before you leave today, you should be able to

- Log in to Google, Meet, Calendly and the workspace.
- Say in a sentence what we mean by "teach the why".
- Name the four methods and what each one is for.
- Find a student's folder, and say what goes in each of its six subfolders.
- Say what an `awaiting-…` folder is, and the one thing you never do with it.
- Know who to ask about pay, about teaching, and about anything that goes to a student.

## Later, not today

These are real parts of the job. You'll meet them in your first weeks, not on day one:

- Running your own session from a run sheet.
- Writing a run sheet and a session record.
- Drafting a recap or homework email for Braden to approve.
- Grading with Claude in the workspace (Priya shows you).
- The package window and the money-back guarantee in practice (`policies.md` has the short version).
- The rebate and the exit call after a student's exam.
- Refunds, payments and anything in `finance/` (Marcus only).
- Making new sheets in `studio/` (Braden only).

## Your first week, roughly

Braden sets the pace for each new tutor, so this is the usual shape, not a promise: shadow two or three more sessions, grade with Priya checking, then run a session with Priya on the call, then your first one on your own.
