# Tools and logins

One job: each tool a tutor uses, what it's for, how to get into it step by step, and who sets up your access. **No password is ever written down: not in these notes, not in the workspace, not in a chat message or an email.** Marcus sends invites; you choose your own passwords and keep them in your own password manager.

## The tools, in the order you'll set them up

| Tool | What it's for | Who sets you up |
|---|---|---|
| **Google account** (Steady Dose) | your work email, Chat, Calendar, Meet and Drive, all in one login | Marcus sends the invite before day one |
| **Google Chat** | messaging the team | comes with your Google account |
| **Google Meet** | every session; notes are made from the session audio | comes with your Google account |
| **Calendly** | students book sessions on your booking page | Marcus makes your page |
| **The workspace** (opened in Obsidian) | the shared folder that runs the business: students, the loop, the library | Marcus adds you to the shared drive |
| **Claude in the workspace** | grades against the key, drafts run sheets and records, answers "where is…" | Marcus sets up your access; not on day one |

Claude in the workspace is not the onboarder you chat with on day one. The onboarder has only its own notes and can't see the workspace. You don't use Claude in the workspace today; Priya shows you in your first weeks.

## Getting set up, step by step

This is part 1 of your first day. Do the five in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

### 1. Your Google account

1. Find the invite email. Marcus sent it to your personal email before day one. It has your new Steady Dose address and a link to sign in.
2. Open the link, set your own password, and save it in your password manager.
3. You're in when you can see your Steady Dose inbox in Gmail.

### 2. Google Chat

1. In Gmail, open Chat on the left side, or go to chat.google.com.
2. Marcus has added you to the team space. Open it and say hi to Braden, Marcus and Priya.
3. To message one person, start a chat with their name.

### 3. Google Calendar and Meet

1. Open Google Calendar. Each part of today is on it, with its time.
2. To join anything on Meet, click the event, then "Join with Google Meet". There's no separate link to keep; it's always in the calendar event.
3. Before you join, Meet shows you a preview. Check your camera and microphone there.

### 4. Calendly

1. Marcus emails you the link to your booking page.
2. Open it and check that your name is right. That's all for today: the page stays off until Marcus turns it on, when you're ready to take students.

### 5. The workspace, in Obsidian

Obsidian is a free notes app. It opens a folder of plain text pages and shows them as a list you can click through. The workspace is that folder. Obsidian is only the window you read it in.

1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in with your Steady Dose account. The shared drive called Steady Dose now shows up on your computer like any other folder.
2. Install Obsidian from obsidian.md. It's free, and you don't need an Obsidian account.
3. Open Obsidian, choose "Open folder as vault", and pick the `steady-dose` folder inside the shared drive.
4. The list of folders on the left matches the map in `the-workspace.md`. Click any page to read it. The magnifying glass searches every page for a word.

On day one you read, and you make one new page: your grading of the practice homework. To make it, right-click the practice student's `grading/` folder, choose "New note", and name it `practice-1-` followed by your first name.

## What you won't have, and why

| Tool | Who uses it | Why not you |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** (payments and refunds) | Marcus | money is one person's job, so nothing is paid or refunded by mistake |
| **The shared inbox** | Marcus and Braden | what a student sends lands in their folder in the workspace, so you don't need the inbox |
| **The website and social accounts** | Braden | public posts follow rules a new tutor doesn't need yet |

## Where homework comes in

Students send homework to the homework email address on their welcome email (Marcus has it if you ever need it). It's pulled into the workspace and lands in that student's `homework-returned/` folder, so you never need to dig through an inbox for it. If a student says they sent it and it isn't there, tell Marcus.

## If something doesn't work

For a login, Drive or Obsidian, tell Marcus on Chat. If it's your Google login that fails, so you can't get into Chat, reply to the invite email Marcus sent you. If you can't find the invite email at all, check your spam folder, then reply to the email Braden sent you with your start time. Don't make a second account, and don't borrow someone else's login.
