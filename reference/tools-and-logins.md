# Tools and logins

One job: each tool a tutor uses, what it's for, and who sets up your access. **No password is ever written down: not in these notes, not in the workspace, not in a chat message or an email.** Marcus sends invites; you choose your own passwords and keep them in your own password manager.

## In the order you'll set them up

| Tool | What it's for | Who sets you up | You on day one |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google account** (Steady Dose) | your work email, Chat, Drive and Calendar, all in one login | Marcus sends the invite before day one | log in first; everything else hangs off it |
| **Google Chat** | messaging the team | comes with your Google account | say hi to Braden, Marcus and Priya |
| **Google Meet** | every session; notes are made from the session audio | comes with your Google account | join Priya's session from the calendar invite |
| **Calendly** | students book sessions on your booking page | Marcus makes your page | look at it; Marcus turns it on when you're ready to take students |
| **The workspace** (opened in Obsidian) | the shared folder that runs the business: students, the loop, the library | Marcus shares the folder; you install Obsidian (free) and open it | follow `the-workspace.md` |
| **Claude** | works inside the workspace: grades against the key, drafts run sheets and records, answers "where is…" | Marcus sets up your access | ask it where things are; you grade by hand today, and Priya shows you grading with Claude in your first weeks |

The Claude inside the workspace is not the onboarder you chat with on day one. The onboarder has only its own notes and can't see the workspace.

## What you won't have, and why

| Tool | Who uses it | Why not you |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** (payments and refunds) | Marcus | money is one person's job, so nothing is paid or refunded by mistake |
| **The shared inbox** | Marcus and Braden | student emails reach you through the workspace, after Braden approves them |
| **The website and social accounts** | Braden | public posts follow rules a new tutor doesn't need yet |

## Where homework comes in

Students send homework to the homework email address on their welcome email (Marcus has it if you ever need it). It's pulled into the workspace and lands in that student's `homework-returned/` folder, so you never need to dig through an inbox for it. If a student says they sent it and it isn't there, tell Marcus.

## If a login doesn't work

Tell Marcus on Chat. Don't make a second account, and don't borrow someone else's login.
