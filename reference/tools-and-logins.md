# Tools and logins

One job: each tool a tutor uses, what it's for, what's already set up for you, and what you do with it on day one. **No password is ever written down: not in these notes, not in the workspace, not in a chat message or an email.** You choose your own passwords and keep them in your own password manager.

## What's set up for you

This is part 1 of your first day. Marcus set up all five before you started, and his welcome email, sent to your personal address, has your sign-in invite. Nothing needs installing.

| Tool | What it's for | What's set up | What you do today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google account** (Steady Dose) | your work email, Chat, Calendar, Meet and Drive, all in one login | Your address is your first name, then `@fake-steadydose.example` | Sign in and choose your own password. Save it in your password manager; the one built into your browser or phone is fine. |
| **Google Chat** | messaging the team | You're in the team space, with Braden, Marcus and Priya | Open Chat from the left side of Gmail and say hi in the team space. To message one person, start a chat with their name. |
| **Google Calendar and Meet** | every session is on your calendar and held on Meet; notes are made from the session audio | Tomorrow's session is on your calendar: "Shadow Priya's session", 10:00 AM Pacific | Find the event. You don't join anything today. |
| **Calendly** | students book sessions on your booking page | Your booking page is made, and it's switched off | Nothing. Marcus turns it on when you're ready to take students. |
| **The workspace** | the shared folder the business runs on: every student's folder, and the library of sheets | You've been added to the shared drive, `steady-dose`, in Google Drive under "Shared drives" | Open it and look around. Part 3 of the day is the tour (`the-workspace.md`). |

## Joining a session, from day two

A session's Meet link is always in its calendar event. Click the event, then "Join with Google Meet". Meet shows you a preview before you join, and that's where you check your camera and microphone.

## What you won't have, and why

| Tool | Who uses it | Why not you |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** (payments and refunds) | Marcus | money is one person's job, so nothing is paid or refunded by mistake |
| **The shared inbox** | Marcus and Braden | what a student sends lands in their folder in the workspace, so you don't need the inbox |
| **The website and social accounts** | Braden | public posts follow rules a new tutor doesn't need yet |

## Where homework comes in

Students send homework to the homework email address on their welcome email (Marcus has it if you ever need it). It's pulled into the workspace and lands in that student's `homework-returned/` folder, so you never need to dig through an inbox for it. If a student says they sent it and it isn't there, tell Marcus.

## If something doesn't work

For a login or the shared drive, tell Marcus on Chat. If you can't get into Chat, because the welcome email never came or your Google login fails, check your spam folder first, then email him from your personal address at `marcus@fake-steadydose.example`. Nothing else on day one waits for it. Don't make a second account, and don't borrow someone else's login.
