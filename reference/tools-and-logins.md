# Tools and logins

One job: each tool a tutor uses, what it's for, how to get into it step by step, and who sets up your access. **No password is ever written down: not in these notes, not in the workspace, not in a chat message or an email.** Marcus sends invites; you choose your own passwords and keep them in your own password manager.

## The tools, in the order you'll set them up

| Tool | What it's for | Who set it up for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Google account** (Steady Dose) | your work email, Chat, Calendar, Meet and Drive, all in one login | Marcus sent the welcome email before day one |
| **Google Chat** | messaging the team | comes with your Google account |
| **Google Meet** | every session; notes are made from the session audio | comes with your Google account |
| **Calendly** | students book sessions on your booking page | Marcus made your page before day one |
| **The workspace** (a shared drive in Google Drive) | the shared folder that runs the business: students, the loop, the library | Marcus added you to the shared drive before day one |
| **Claude in the workspace** | grades against the key, drafts run sheets and records, answers "where is…" | Marcus, in your first weeks; not on day one |

Claude in the workspace is not the onboarder you chat with on day one. The onboarder has only its own notes and can't see the workspace. You don't use Claude in the workspace today; Priya shows you in your first weeks.

## Getting set up, step by step

This is part 1 of your first day. Do the five in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

If you're trying this out without a real account, you can't open the fake links. Tell the onboarder you've done a step and it will tell you what you'd see, from the "what you see" line under each one.

### 1. Your Google account

1. Open the welcome email and click link 1, `https://accounts.fake-steadydose.example/accept-invite`.
2. Set your own password, and save it in your password manager (the one built into your browser or phone is fine).

What you see: your Steady Dose inbox in Gmail, with one message in it, a copy of the welcome email.

### 2. Google Chat

1. In Gmail, open Chat on the left side, or click link 2, `https://chat.fake-steadydose.example/team`.
2. Say hi in the team space.
3. To message one person, start a chat with their name.

What you see: the team space, with Braden, Marcus and Priya in it, and a pinned welcome note from Marcus.

### 3. Google Calendar and Meet

1. Open Google Calendar.
2. From day two, to join a session on Meet, click its event, then "Join with Google Meet". A session's Meet link is always in its calendar event.
3. Meet shows you a preview before you join. That's where you check your camera and microphone.

What you see: an empty calendar today, and one event tomorrow, "Shadow Priya's session", at 10:00 AM Pacific, with link 5 in it, `https://meet.fake-steadydose.example/day-two-shadow`. You don't join anything today.

### 4. Calendly

1. Click link 3, `https://calendly.fake-steadydose.example/your-first-name`.
2. Check that your name is right. That's all for today.

What you see: your booking page, with a note that it isn't taking bookings yet. Marcus turns it on when you're ready to take students.

### 5. The workspace

The workspace is one shared folder: folders, and plain text pages inside them. There's nothing to install.

1. Click link 4, `https://drive.fake-steadydose.example/steady-dose`. It opens in Google Drive in your browser.
2. Click a folder to open it, and click a page to read it. The search box at the top finds any word.

What you see: the top of the workspace, which matches the map in `the-workspace.md`: three pages (`AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `STATUS.md`) and six folders (`_config/`, `_templates/`, `operations/`, `sales/`, `marketing/`, `finance/`).

On day one you only read the workspace. Your practice grading is done with the onboarder, in the chat, so there's nothing to save.

## What you won't have, and why

| Tool | Who uses it | Why not you |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** (payments and refunds) | Marcus | money is one person's job, so nothing is paid or refunded by mistake |
| **The shared inbox** | Marcus and Braden | what a student sends lands in their folder in the workspace, so you don't need the inbox |
| **The website and social accounts** | Braden | public posts follow rules a new tutor doesn't need yet |

## Where homework comes in

Students send homework to the homework email address on their welcome email (Marcus has it if you ever need it). It's pulled into the workspace and lands in that student's `homework-returned/` folder, so you never need to dig through an inbox for it. If a student says they sent it and it isn't there, tell Marcus.

## If something doesn't work

For a login or the shared drive, tell Marcus on Chat. If it's your Google login that fails, so you can't get into Chat, reply to his welcome email. If you can't find the welcome email in your personal inbox, check your spam folder; the onboarder can show you its copy meanwhile. Don't make a second account, and don't borrow someone else's login.
